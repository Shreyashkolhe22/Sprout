Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Sprout is a habit tracker and notes app. The short version: your data stays on your phone.

What Sprout stores

  • The name you type so Pip knows what to call you.
  • Your habits and the days you completed them.
  • Your notes, and copies of any images or PDFs you attach to them.

All of this is saved only in Sprout's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read it.

What Sprout does not do

  • It has no accounts and no servers.
  • It collects no personal data, analytics or crash reports.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or tracking libraries.
  • It makes no network requests. The app works fully offline.

Files you attach

When you attach an image or PDF, Android's own picker lets you choose it, and Sprout can only see the files you pick. A copy is saved inside the app. Removing the attachment or deleting the note deletes that copy.

Permissions

Sprout asks for no special permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or the internet.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit or note inside the app, or uninstall Sprout to remove everything. Logging out keeps your data on the device so it is there when you log back in with the same name.

Children

Sprout collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
