Questions

Is Sprout really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Does it need an account or internet?

No. There's nothing to sign up for, and it works fully offline. You only enter a name so Pip knows what to call you.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Habits, notes and attachments never leave your device, and uninstalling Sprout removes them. If you log out, your data stays saved under your name until you uninstall.

Why does Android warn me before installing?

Android shows that prompt for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Sprout is signed with its own release key, and you can verify the file with the SHA-256 checksum above.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 7.0 or newer. There's no iPhone version yet.

Where can I report a bug or see the code?

On GitHub. Open an issue and say what happened.
